AdSelect reports temporary website maintenance

Update: AdSelect administrators say the main adselect.net domain was temporarily unavailable due to website maintenance.

According to communications on AdSelect's Telegram channels, adselect.org and adselect.biz remained available as alternative access domains.

Administrators say existing accounts remain valid, users do not need to register again, and withdrawal arrangements remain unchanged.

They have asked users to await the scheduled Thursday, 12 November, withdrawals.

Important: These are statements attributed to the administrators. Successful withdrawals have not yet been independently verified.

Takeaway: A maintenance announcement is not proof of fraud, but neither is it proof of payment reliability. Verify before trusting.
